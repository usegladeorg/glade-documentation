  # Notifications
  
  Glade uses notifications to tell you when something on your account needs your attention. You don't have to keep checking the app to stay informed.
  
  ---
  
  ## Kinds of notification
  
  | Kind | You get one when |
  |---|---|
  | Transfer | A payment you sent or received has settled or failed |
  | Policy | One of your agents' spend policies has blocked a payment or is waiting for your approval |
  | Security | There's been a sign-in, or a key or wallet on your account has changed |
  | System | Glade posts a platform-wide announcement unrelated to any particular payment |
  
  Each notification shows its kind as a label. That makes it easy to separate the ones you need to act on (usually Policy) from the ones that are only informational.
  
  ---
  
  ## Working through the list
  
  - A notification you haven't read has a filled dot and a highlighted background.
  - Opening one marks it read.
  - **Mark all read** clears every unread notification at once.
  - The **All** and **Unread** tabs at the top switch between the full list and just the unread ones.
  
  ---
  
  ## Turning kinds on or off
  
  Below the list, **Notification preferences** has a separate toggle for each rule. Switching one off stops new notifications of that kind from being created. Anything already in your list stays there, and your account keeps working exactly as before.
  
  Be clear about one thing in particular. If you turn off Policy notifications, your agents' spend policies are not relaxed in any way. Glade just no longer tells you each time a policy blocks or holds a payment, and enforcement carries on regardless. For how enforcement works, read [Spending rules](../agents/spending-rules.md).
