  # Topping up
  
  Open the Wallet screen to see your balance, the address behind it, and the ways money comes in and goes out.
  
  ---
  
  ## Your spendable balance
  
  The big figure at the top is how much USDG your primary account can spend right now. Below it sits the Robinhood Chain address connected to your account.
  
  No address linked yet? You'll see a prompt to add one in [Preferences](preferences.md). External payouts won't work until you do.
  
  ---
  
  ## Adding funds
  
  Press **Top up** to put USDG into your primary account. The flow goes like this:
  
  1. Pick a preset amount ($10, $25, $50 or $100) or type your own.
  2. Continue. Glade gives you a single-use deposit address on Robinhood Chain.
  3. Send USDG to that address from any exchange or EVM wallet. Bridged USDC works too, and usually shows up in around two seconds.
  4. Once you've sent it, tap **Transferred**.
  
  From there, Glade watches the chain for the incoming deposit, which typically arrives within a few seconds. When it lands, the balance updates and the panel closes by itself, so there's no need to refresh or wonder whether it went through.
  
  ---
  
  ## The numbers under your balance
  
  | Figure | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Spent this month | Settled outgoing volume from the 1st of the current month onward |
  | Total topped up | Everything this account has received from outside Glade |
  | Avg settlement time | How long, on average, your settled payments took to become final |
  
  ---
  
  ## Recent activity
  
  A short list of the latest payments on your primary account sits on the Wallet screen. Each one shows who it was with, the memo and the time. Incoming money has a down arrow and outgoing money has an up arrow. For a complete view across all your accounts, with filters and receipts, go to [History](history.md).
  
  Glade doesn't deduct a network fee from what you send. Whatever amount you enter is exactly what arrives, down to the cent.
