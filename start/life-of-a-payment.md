  # Life of a payment
  
  A Glade account is made of two parts. The first is a smart account on Robinhood Chain that you custody yourself. The second is a set of confidential token contracts that keep the amount of every payment secret. Your keys stay with you, settlement completes in well under a second, and the amount is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere.
  
  Put simply, anyone can see who paid whom, but nobody else can see how much.
