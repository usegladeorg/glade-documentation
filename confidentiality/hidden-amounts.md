  # Hidden amounts
  
  Glade keeps the size of a payment private while leaving the parties visible. On Robinhood Chain, anyone can see your address, but the numbers moving through it are encrypted. The network still checks every transfer for validity. It just can't read what any of them are worth.
  
  This page walks through how that works.
